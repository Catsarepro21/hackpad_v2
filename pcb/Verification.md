# Verification of simple build with upgrade pads

KiCad 10.0.6: zero reported DRC violations (including warnings), zero unconnected items, zero schematic parity issues; ERC zero reported violations. Reports in the local release download preserve the configured checks for transparency. Existing LED aperture and fine-pitch converter clearance rules are retained; general clearances were not relaxed to pass.

All 71 original footprints retain their library ID, position, rotation and PCB side. Seven solder jumpers were added. All 221 netlisted pin connections match the PCB. Fourteen optional references carry DNP flags in both schematic and PCB. Logical checks passed for both jumper configurations; these checks do not simulate the components or replace electrical measurements. The mechanical correction changes 3D-model transforms rather than footprint placement or electrical geometry.

Fresh manufacturing exports reproduce the published Gerber/drill package after excluding only creation-date metadata. Both copper, paste, silkscreen and mask layers, board edges, and separate plated/nonplated drill files are included. All optional copper pads are present for later assembly.

The plate datum was corrected: the 1.5 mm plate spans z=5.1–6.6 mm above PCB bottom, with the nominal PCB top at z=1.6 mm and 3.5 mm integral spacers between it and the plate. The 90 x 84 mm XY outline and mounting coordinates are unchanged; raising the plate by 1.6 mm increases the overall case height from 23.4 to 25.0 mm. Model origins and nominal socketed XIAO height were corrected. The battery reserve remains 40 x 33 x 7 mm for the 36 x 29 x 4.75 mm battery, with 40.2 mm guide spacing. See the [release audit](../docs/Release-audit.md) and regenerated case/interference reports for the measured nominal-model results.

The U1 model uses a nominal 11.04 mm gap from carrier-board top to module underside: verified 8.50 mm female-socket housing plus assumed 2.54 mm male-header plastic. Exact male-header height, seating and USB plug/cable access remain physical checks. The supplied MX model is approximate; residual plate engagement is not proof of fit for the actual owned switch. Flexible leads, battery restraint, antenna placement, exact encoder model, printed tolerances and complete assembly fit require first-article verification. Solder jumpers are flat copper and are not modeled as separate parts.

Repository firmware was not modified. Its MCP23008 scanner will not operate the simple direct matrix unchanged; GPIO1/5/6 also retain their old RGB-enable/I2C roles in that firmware. Convert it using the pin map in Build-options.md and verify USB/BLE HID on hardware. A separately supplied compiled firmware candidate does not establish hardware operation and is not the unchanged repository firmware. Start RGB at brightness 64/255, keep Wi-Fi off on battery, and measure supply current before increasing brightness. Verify battery connector polarity before connection. Future battery RGB needs the JP7 bypass opened and battery discharge capacity checked.

The independent component and power review is retained in the local release download. The full assembly is not production-qualified; exact part fit, connector seating and hardware/firmware validation remain.
